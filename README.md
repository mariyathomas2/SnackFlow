# SnackFlow – Order-to-Audit Management System

**Live Demo:** https://snackflow-mmns.onrender.com

SnackFlow is a web-based Order-to-Audit Management System designed for a small food manufacturing business. It integrates inventory, purchasing, production, sales, invoicing, dispatch, payment tracking, and costing into a single application.

## Features

- **Role-Based Authentication** – Access control for different business roles.
- **Inventory Management** – Track raw materials and finished goods.
- **Purchase Management** – Record purchases and update stock.
- **Production Management** – Track material consumption, production output, and wastage.
- **Sales Order Management** – Manage retailer and distributor orders.
- **Stock Shortage Detection** – Identify shortages before fulfilling orders.
- **GST Invoice Generation** – Generate invoices with applicable GST.
- **Dispatch Management** – Track product dispatch.
- **Payment Tracking** – Record collections and outstanding balances.
- **Production Costing** – Calculate production cost per unit.
- **Audit & Reporting** – Maintain records of business transactions.
