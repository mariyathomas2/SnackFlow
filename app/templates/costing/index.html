Manufacturing Costing Methodology

SnackFlow calculates unit product cost through Batch Absorption Costing. Under this methodology:

Standard Murukku 200g Benchmark Formula:
Cost per Packet = (Actual Material Consumed + Direct Labour + Gas/Power) / Good Output Packets
  • Benchmark Example (Batch 1):
    Raw Materials (Flour, Oil, Spices, Pouches, Cartons) = ₹3,801.00
    Direct Labour = ₹1,200.00 | Gas & Power = ₹600.00
    Total Batch Cost = ₹5,601.00
    Good Output = 188 packets (Yield Variance: -6%)
    Cost per Packet = ₹5,601 / 188 = ₹29.79
    Selling Price = ₹50.00 → Gross Margin = ₹20.21 / pkt (40.42%).
