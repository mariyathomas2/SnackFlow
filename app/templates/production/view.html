Costing Methodology Explained

SnackFlow utilizes Batch Absorption Costing aligned with Indian packaged food manufacturing standards.

  • Direct Materials: Value of actual consumed ingredients (flour, oil, spice mix) plus utilized packaging pouches and cartons.
  • Scrap & Damaged Packaging: Damaged pouches (6 pcs in benchmark) are recognized in the batch cost and deducted from packaging inventory via MATERIAL_WASTAGE.
  • Direct Conversion Cost: Includes direct labour allocated to frying and pouch packaging, plus dedicated LPG gas and electricity utility expenses.
  • Good Output Allocation: Total accumulated batch cost is divided solely by passed good packets (188 units) to compute the true net cost per marketable packet (₹29.79), ensuring accurate wholesale gross margins.
